# Black frame

Horizontal footage inside a vertical black canvas. Use for screen recordings, slides or a wide shot whose content must remain legible.

Preserve the full screen recording instead of zooming into a small webcam. Keep the fixed hook above the 1080×608 footage region at y=656. Position captions within the video without covering the useful interface.

Implementation: `template.mjs`. Reusable instructions: `skills/format-black-frame/SKILL.md`.
